# Survival App

Offline emergency-preparedness app: supplies checklist and shopping route, water and food calculators, first aid, grid-down playbook, household plan, and a map with water, medical, shelter, fuel and other points anywhere in the U.S.

**Download:** go to [Releases](../../releases/latest) and tap **Survival-App.apk**.

To update the app, replace `app/src/main/assets/index.html` and push. A new APK builds automatically.
